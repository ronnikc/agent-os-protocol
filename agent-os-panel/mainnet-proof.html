Mainnet Proof Pilot

Use Concordium Wallet for Web on Mainnet to prove you are over 18 without revealing your date of birth.

Review the age-range request in your wallet. The site sends the resulting proof and its credential identifier to Concordium’s Mainnet verifier for checking. The pilot does not store the presentation or send a transaction.

This pilot demonstrates native Concordium identity verification. The x401 interoperability test uses a separate OpenID4VP credential exchange.
